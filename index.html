How each bonus is calculated

Quarter-to-date values average the weekly entries. Sales and transaction averages are a simple average of weekly % changes — close, but the official number uses total quarter dollars and counts. "At risk" means within reach of the line but not safely over it. Payouts shown are estimates; the owner's payout sheet is final.
