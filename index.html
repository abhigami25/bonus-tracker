How each bonus is calculated

Quarter-to-date values average your weekly entries. Sales and transaction averages are a simple average of weekly % changes, which is close but not exact; the official number uses total quarter dollars and counts. "At risk" means within reach of the line but not safely over it. This is an estimate to steer the week — your owner's payout sheet is final.
